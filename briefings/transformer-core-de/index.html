Where the loss comes from

Core loss has three parts. Each is a material property.

01 · Hysteresis
Ph ∝ f · loop area
Set by grain structure, texture and stress.
02 · Eddy current
Pe ∝ t²·f²·B² / ρ
Thinner, more resistive material cuts it.
03 · Excess
Pex ∝ (f·B)1.5
Comes from domain-wall motion.
Sources: loss separation after G. Bertotti, IEEE Trans. Magn. 24, 621 (1988); classical eddy-current loss for a sheet of thickness t and resistivity ρ. Diagrams: Mirdyne, schematic.
